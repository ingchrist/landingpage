About Santive

Santive is the premier annual celebration bringing together music, art, culture, and community. Every year we gather thousands of passionate fans for an unforgettable experience filled with world-class performances, art installations, and extraordinary memories.

Founded over a decade ago, Santive has grown into one of the most anticipated events on the calendar — a place where creativity meets celebration. Join us on the 23rd of December, 2024 for an evening you will never forget.
